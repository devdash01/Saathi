1. Executive Summary & Core Innovations

SAATHI is a real-time computer-aided dispatch (CAD) decision-support workstation built specifically for operators of India's 181 Women Helpline and 112 Emergency Response Support System (ERSS). Most traditional emergency AI tries to understand what a caller articulates explicitly. SAATHI is engineered for the caller who cannot say it.

INNOVATION 1 Coded-Call Detection

Under intimate partner confinement, callers pretend to order food ("bhaiya paneer roll bhej do") or call a relative. SAATHI extracts hidden distress indicators, assigns P2 Urgent priority, and restricts operator guidance to safe Yes/No questions only.

INNOVATION 2 Invariant Urgency Floors

Once hard evidence of danger (weapons, active physical violence, life threat) is registered, the priority rating (e.g. P1 Immediate) is locked monotonically. If the caller subsequently claims "sab theek hai" under duress, the urgency floor cannot be lowered.

INNOVATION 3 SafeSignal Protocol

Strict rule filtering (R1–R10) prevents questions that endanger callers. Prohibits abuser names, commands to move, promises, or multi-part questions. Supports 1-touch citizen responses (Haan / Nahi) transmitted directly to the caller's mobile interface.

5. Tactical Fleet Dispatch & Geodesic Proximity Mathematics

Helpline responders are calculated dynamically relative to the caller's live GPS coordinates using the Great-Circle Haversine formula to compute exact ground distance and realistic urban arrival ETAs:

Geodesic Distance (Haversine Formula):
a = sin²(Δφ / 2) + cos(φ₁) · cos(φ₂) · sin²(Δλ / 2)
d = 2 · R · atan2(√a, √(1 − a))   [where R = 6,371 km earth radius]

Dynamic Traffic-Weighted Urban ETA:
ETA (minutes) = round( (d / v_responder) × 60 × T_traffic + T_turnout )
  • 112 Police PCR: v = 32 km/h, T_turnout = 1.5 min
  • 108 ALS Ambulance: v = 28 km/h, T_turnout = 2.0 min
  • 181 Sakhi Shelter: v = 25 km/h, T_turnout = 3.0 min
  • DLSA Legal Aid: v = 22 km/h, T_turnout = 4.0 min

7. The SafeSignal Protocol & Direct Operator Inquiries

When a caller is in distress, standard questioning protocols often provoke further violence. SafeSignal enforces 10 strict invariants across all operator and automated prompts:

The 10 Inviolable Safety Rules (R1–R10)
  1. R1 (Binary Answers): Strictly Yes/No only. No open questions.
  2. R2 (Brevity): Question length must be ≤ 14 words.
  3. R3 (No Abuser Names): Never say "pati", "husband", or abuser names.
  4. R4 (No Weapon Mentions): Never ask "kya unke paas chaku hai".
  5. R5 (No Movement Demands): Never command caller to run or open doors.
  6. R6 (No Volume Commands): Never ask the caller to speak louder.
  7. R7 (No Dangerous Callbacks): Never schedule an unannounced callback.
  8. R8 (Atomic Delivery): Strictly one question at a time.
  9. R9 (Zero PII Ingestion): No asking for Aadhaar, bank details, or IDs.
  10. R10 (No Promises): Never promise police will arrive in 2 minutes.
Direct Operator Inquiry Channel

Operators can type custom questions or select verified presets. Once sent, the question renders directly on the citizen's mobile interface with high-contrast 1-touch response buttons:

10. Operational Impact & Deployment Readiness

By reducing emergency triage latency from the national average of 3.5 minutes down to under 10 seconds, SAATHI prevents vital information loss during dropped or intercepted calls. Its quote-grounded evidence links eliminate operator cognitive bias, while its deterministic safety invariants ensure that women in the most acute danger—those forced to speak in whispers or codes—receive the fastest, safest, and most coordinated response possible across Indian emergency services.
